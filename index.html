About RTL Capital

BUILT FOR
REAL
TRADERS.

RTL Capital was created with one goal: give people a clear, no-fluff path to learning how to trade stocks. Whether you want to swing trade around your day job or day trade full-time, the program meets you where you are.

The lessons are video-based, structured around how the market actually moves — not how textbooks say it should. Every module is built around real setups, real decisions, and real outcomes.

The community lives on Discord, where members share watchlists, post trade reviews, ask questions, and hold each other accountable. Trading is isolating — this is built to change that.

R
Reed
Founder — RTL Capital
Reed started RTL Capital to share what he's learned through years of trading stocks — the setups that work, the mistakes that cost money, and the mindset that ties it all together. The program is built on the belief that good trading can be taught to anyone willing to put in the work.
What we stand for
Transparency over hype
No fake screenshots, no "I turned $500 into $50k" promises. Just honest trading education.
Process over outcome
A losing trade with good process is better than a winning trade with a bad one. We teach the process.
Simplicity over complexity
The best traders use simple systems. We cut out the noise and focus on what actually matters.
Community over solo hustle
You'll improve faster with people around you. The Discord is a core part of what RTL Capital offers.

No. Trading 101 starts from zero — what a stock is, how to read a chart, everything. You can join with no experience and follow the courses in order.

After checkout on Whop, you'll get instant access to the Discord server and all video content. Everything is available immediately.
Can I cancel my quarterly subscription? +
Yes — quarterly members can cancel anytime through Whop. No questions asked. Yearly members keep access for the full 12 months regardless.

The curriculum is broker-agnostic. Popular choices include Webull, thinkorswim (TD Ameritrade), and Interactive Brokers — but you can follow along with whatever you use.

No. RTL Capital is an education program. The goal is to teach you how to find and execute your own trades. Reed shares his trades for educational purposes, not as financial advice.
